# Business Glossary -- Saul Stitch House

Saul Stitch House is a monogramming / embroidery gift business selling
personalized goods (handkerchiefs, towels, baby items, accessories, holiday
pieces) through **Shopify** (own storefront, saulstitchhouse.com) and
**Etsy** (marketplace), with **QuickBooks Online** as the accounting system
of record.

## Terms

- **Sales channel** -- Shopify or Etsy. Where an order was placed.
- **Gross revenue** -- Order total before channel fees are subtracted
  (includes shipping and tax collected).
- **Net revenue** -- Gross revenue minus channel fees (Shopify payment
  processing, Etsy transaction/listing/payment fees).
- **Channel fees** -- What Shopify or Etsy charge to process a sale. Etsy
  fees are typically higher (~6.5% effective) than Shopify's own payments
  processing (~2.9%), though Etsy also includes marketing/ad fees for some
  sellers.
- **Estimated margin** -- Line revenue minus `unit_cost * quantity`, where
  `unit_cost` comes from whichever system (QuickBooks or Shopify) has it
  recorded for that SKU. This is a **blended per-unit cost**, not a true
  bill-of-materials cost -- see `known_gaps.md`.
- **AOV (average order value)** -- Gross revenue divided by order count.
- **Reconciliation** -- Matching a channel's reported payout to the actual
  bank deposit recorded in QuickBooks, to confirm money that was supposed to
  arrive actually did.
- **LTV (lifetime value)** -- Total gross/net revenue from a customer across
  all their orders, all channels, all time.
- **Repeat customer** -- A customer with more than one order. Notable in this
  business because wedding vendors, planners, and boutiques sometimes place
  multiple separate orders.
- **Product line** -- The `product_type` on a SKU: Wedding, Home, Baby,
  Accessories, Holiday (matches the site's navigation categories).

## Who reads this dashboard

The primary user is the business owner, not an accountant or a data analyst.
Answers should be plain-English, lead with the number and what it means for
the business, and flag uncertainty (estimated fees, best-effort matching,
sample data) rather than presenting everything as equally precise.
